BFMXR Library

The 16 sounds on your FM-1: play them, save them to your computer as .bfxr (opens in bfxr.net) or .wav, and send .bfxr files back. Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB and running BFMXR.

    • On the FM-1: hold SAVE and press a white key to store a sound in that slot; hold SEL and press a white key to recall it.
    • "Send" puts a .bfxr file into a slot (or into the sound being edited) and keeps it on the FM-1 at once: a sound from bfxr.net's Bfxr or any of its other synths the FM-1 has (press ENV on the FM-1 for the list).
    • The .wav files are rendered here by bfxr's own code, at 44.1 kHz.
